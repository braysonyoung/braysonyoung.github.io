Artist Management

Kassa Overall

Through an ongoing employment opportunity with Kassa Overall, a Grammy-nominated artist and producer in Seattle, I am gaining experience in artist management and brand marketing. I assist in the development of promotional strategies, work out tour logistics, and produce content for multiple social media platforms. This experience is providing valuable insight into the music industry and the importance of effective collaboration in the creative process. As you will see below, I am also building upon my data-analysis skills by tracking and analyzing multiple social media metrics and relating them to content type and audience engagement. This allows me to make informed decisions about marketing strategies and learn for future projects.

Marketing Content

I create both short and long-form videos to be used as promotional clips, behind-the-scenes footage, and music videos for Kassa Overall. Many instances require me to stitch together multiple angles of footage in both studio and live environments to create cohesive and engaging content. This portion of the job allows me to build my video editing skills, refine my creative "vision", and see how an industry professional handles their brand identity. Every image on this page is part of the videography and photo shoots I work with that go out to socials.

Tour Management

Another aspect of my internship is assisting with planning and executing shows and tours. I help coordinate logistics, look at schedules, and set up equipment for live performances. This experience allows me to see the behind-the-scenes work that goes into a successful tour and the importance of communication and organization in the music industry.

More about Kassa Overall

Kassa Overall is a Seattle-based Grammy-nominated drummer, producer, rapper, and bandleader known for pushing the boundaries between jazz, hip-hop, and avant-garde music. A graduate of the Oberlin Conservatory of Music, Overall has spent more than two decades working at the highest levels of the jazz world, touring and recording with artists including Geri Allen, Jon Batiste, Vijay Iyer, Steve Coleman, Gary Bartz, Matt Wong, and Terri Lyne Carrington.

Overall earned a Grammy nomination for Best Jazz Instrumental Album for his work with Terri Lyne Carrington on Waiting Game at the 63rd Grammy Awards. In 2025, he was selected as one of six recipients of the prestigious Doris Duke Artist Award, recognizing artists whose work demonstrates exceptional creativity and impact. His 2023 album ANIMALS appeared on year-end lists from major outlets including The New York Times, NPR, MOJO, the San Francisco Chronicle, and The Seattle Times.

Beyond his awards, Overall has established himself as a true multi-instrumentalist and musical polymath, combining virtuosic drumming, beat-making, rapping, production, and composition into a distinct sound. His collaborations span jazz, hip-hop, and alternative music, with artists such as Danny Brown, Lil B, Nick Hakim, Shabazz Palaces, and Vijay Iyer. His work has earned descriptions from Pitchfork as that of "one of modern jazz music's most audacious futurists," while Grammy-winning drummer Terri Lyne Carrington has called him a "pre-eminent style bender and blender."
