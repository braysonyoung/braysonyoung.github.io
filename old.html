While some pieces are created to promote his own performances, much of Brayson's poster work comes from a desire to experiment and support the creative communities around him. Through creative imagery and composition, he uses each design as an opportunity to explore new ideas and develop his visual language. Like songwriting, the process often begins with an instinctive idea before evolving through experimentation and refinement. For Young, posters are not simply promotional materials, but another way to build atmosphere, tell a story, and connect people with music.

“Visual design has become an extension of my relationship with music.”

When asked about his approach to visual work, Young described digital design as another form of storytelling. “The content I create is always connected to music - whether for my own shows, projects, or performances from other artists. Even when I’m designing for someone else, I’m trying to understand the energy of the event and translate that into something visual.”

"Creating social media content is a way to capture the feeling of a performance before anyone even hears the first note."
